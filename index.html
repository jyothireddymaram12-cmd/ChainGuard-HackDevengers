03 / THREAT SIGNALS

What ChainGuard looks for

🎁

Fake rewards

Prize, cashback, lottery, refund and “you won” claims designed to trigger excitement.

⏱

Urgency

Pressure such as “act now”, “within 5 minutes”, or “offer expires today”.

📣

Viral forwarding

Requests to forward to 5, 10, or more contacts or groups to unlock a benefit.

🔐

Credential requests

Requests for OTPs, PINs, passwords, CVV, card details or account verification.

💳

Payment pressure

Unexpected fees, transfers, deposits, QR payments or “pay to receive” instructions.

🔗

Suspicious links

Unusual domains, shortened URLs, fake login pages and reward-claim links.
